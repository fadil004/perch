Perch — Privacy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Perch is a web app that shows the Eagle reference library you keep in your own Google Drive. It runs entirely in your browser. Perch has no server of its own, and its developer receives none of your data: not your files, not your account details, not usage statistics.

What Perch accesses in your Google account

  • Read-only access to your Google Drive files — to find your Eagle library and show its images, videos and their details.
  • Your Google account email address — used only on your device, to recognise which account is connected.
  • Creating and changing files Perch itself made (optional, asked the first time you save something) — a folder named “Perch Inbox” in your Drive, where the links and videos you share from your phone, and delete requests, wait for Eagle on your computer.

Where your data goes

Directly between your browser and Google’s servers, and nowhere else. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. Perch does not sell, share or transfer your Google data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

Perch’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is kept on your device

  • A Google access token, which expires after about an hour.
  • A cached copy of your library’s index, thumbnails and recently played videos, so it opens quickly.
  • Videos you shared that are still waiting to upload to your Drive.

Signing out in Perch, or clearing the site’s data in your browser, removes them.

The optional phone downloader

If you install it, it runs on your own phone. A link you share is downloaded there, straight from the site it comes from, and uploaded to your own Drive. A cookies file you add stays on the phone. The Google access token it uses for the upload is held in memory only and is never written to disk.

Other services

  • Perch’s files are hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may log requests (such as your IP address).
  • If a saved reference is a YouTube or Vimeo link, its player is loaded from that site when you open it.

Removing Perch’s access

Choose Sign out in Perch, or remove Perch at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the “Perch Inbox” folder from your Drive at any time.
